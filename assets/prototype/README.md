## Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a portrait destination photograph inside a refined iPhone navigation concept demo, not a UI mockup. Create a beautiful photorealistic editorial travel photograph of a small hidden courtyard in an imagined Ann Arbor, Michigan. A modest weathered limestone fountain with a shallow round basin and a slim central water jet sits beyond three broad stone steps, framed by climbing ivy, an old red brick university-town wall and quiet green trees. Dappled late-afternoon sunlight, warm cream stone, muted sage foliage, naturally rippling pale water, slight authentic film texture. Empty, peaceful, intimate and believable, not a grand European palace or a tourist landmark. Eye-level 35mm camera, vertical 2:3 composition with the fountain clearly in the middle third and steps in the lower third, natural breathing room at the top for a future interface overlay. Excellent real material detail. No text, labels, graphic overlays, borders, people, device frame, logos or watermarks. This is a single photographic image, not a collage.
